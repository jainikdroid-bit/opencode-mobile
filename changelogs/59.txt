v0.14.0 — at home without Tailscale or a VPN
- At home, no Tailscale or VPN needed: a computer paired over Tailscale also answers on its home-network address, and the app uses it whenever the phone is on that network (it checks first that it is really your computer).
- Pairing again with a newer link updates the computer instead of adding a second copy.
- Installer: --no-lan turns the home-network address off. Needs the installer run again on the computer.
